Photo Handling

Uploaded reference photos are processed in your browser session and are not sent to the app server by default.

Diagnostics and Analytics

The app may use Vercel Analytics and Speed Insights in production to understand usage and performance trends.

If configured with VITE_SENTRY_DSN, the app can send runtime error telemetry to Sentry.

Local Storage

Session preferences such as mode, durations, and class plan settings are stored in your browser using local storage.
